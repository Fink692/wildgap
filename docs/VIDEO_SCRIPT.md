# WildGap 66-second product cut

This final demo focuses on the deployed product and technical evidence. It intentionally claims no completed Winnipeg outing and no independent human usability study.

## Structure

- **0:00–0:13 — Hook and search:** establish the biodiversity-data gap, product promise, global location search, and radius control.
- **0:13–0:28 — Analysis:** show comparable H3 cells, recent-versus-baseline GBIF coverage, survey-priority language, confidence, and ranking suppression.
- **0:28–0:46 — Timing and mission:** show Open-Meteo context, responsive explorer views, and the printable 60-minute protocol.
- **0:46–0:57 — Account-free sharing:** show completion, portable validated links, mobile field card, and the clearly labeled Winnipeg demo snapshot.
- **0:57–1:06 — Architecture and close:** show the deterministic stack and repeat the core call to action.
