# Public-voting outreach kit

## Short post

Nature data has blind spots. I built **WildGap** to turn uneven biodiversity observations into safe, shareable field missions for students and citizen scientists. It combines real GBIF records, weather context and transparent geospatial scoring—without pretending observations equal wildlife populations.

Try the Winnipeg demo, create a mission near you, and vote only if you think it earns it: **https://wildgap-habitat-2026.fink692.chatgpt.site**

## Guardrails

- Share only after the official public-voting period opens.
- Do not buy votes, automate outreach or pressure recipients.
- Ask for feedback first and votes second.
- Never imply endorsement by GBIF, Open-Meteo, UNEP, a school or a conservation group.
- Keep the submitted deployment stable during judging unless organizers explicitly allow fixes.
