# Winnipeg pilot and usability protocol

## Safety gate

- Choose a public, permitted location during daylight.
- Check weather, closures, transportation and emergency contact details.
- Do not publish sensitive species coordinates, disturb wildlife or enter private property.
- A minor should follow guardian, school and event policies and should not survey alone where unsafe.

## Field mission

1. Generate a Winnipeg mission from live data and save screenshots of its timestamp and explanation.
2. Record the start time, route or public site, weather and target group.
3. Observe for exactly 60 minutes with one consistent platform and method.
4. Submit documentable observations to iNaturalist, eBird or a relevant program.
5. Complete the WildGap mission and attach the public evidence URL when appropriate.
6. Record only defensible outcomes: mission completed, priority cell visited, observation links added.

## Two usability sessions

Give each tester this task without coaching:

> “Use WildGap to find a survey candidate near Winnipeg and create a field mission you could carry out.”

Record:

- Time from page open to mission card
- Whether they can explain “survey priority” in their own words
- Whether they understand the confidence label and data-status banner
- Any hesitation at search, map selection, date selection or sharing
- One thing they expected but could not find

Acceptance: both testers finish within three minutes and neither interprets the score as wildlife abundance. Fix the highest-impact failure before recording the demo.
